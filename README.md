# 🍔 Food Delivery Analytics Dashboard

 📊 Project Overview

The **Food Delivery Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze food delivery business performance across revenue, orders, customers, restaurants, cuisines, and delivery operations.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of key business KPIs and allows users to analyze performance by **city, restaurant, customer type, cuisine, cost category, and year**.

The main objective of this project is to transform food delivery data into meaningful business insights that can help organizations understand customer behavior, restaurant performance, revenue trends, and delivery efficiency.

---
🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall food delivery business performance.
- Monitor total revenue and total orders.
- Analyze average order value.
- Track average delivery time.
- Identify cities generating the highest revenue.
- Analyze restaurant performance.
- Compare new and repeat customers.
- Understand customer ordering behavior.
- Analyze cuisine-wise order distribution.
- Monitor delivery and cancellation performance.
- Identify cities with higher delivery times.
- Analyze restaurant ratings and revenue.
- Provide interactive filtering for business analysis.

---

 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

 📈 Key Business Insights

Based on the dashboard:

Revenue & Orders

The business generates approximately **165M in total revenue** from around **200K orders**.

Customer Retention

Repeat customers represent approximately **50.28%** of the customer base, showing a significant level of returning customer activity.

Delivery Performance

The average delivery time is approximately **44.5 minutes**, while the dashboard indicates that around **48.16% of orders are late**.

This suggests that delivery efficiency is an important area for improvement.

 City Performance

The dashboard shows significant differences in revenue and order volumes between cities. Some locations contribute considerably more revenue than others.

 Restaurant Performance

The dashboard provides a comparison of restaurants based on:

- Revenue
- Number of orders
- Average rating
- Customer activity

Cuisine Analysis

North Indian- cuisine appears among the highest-order categories, followed by categories such as Cafe, Chinese, Biryani, Andhra, Bakery, South Indian, Continental, Desserts, and Pizza.
